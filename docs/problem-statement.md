# Problem Statement

## L1 — Container Congestion Predictor & Port Operations Optimiser

### The Challenge

Container ports worldwide face a growing operational crisis. As global trade volumes increase, vessel arrivals are outpacing infrastructure expansion. Port operators must make critical real-time decisions with inadequate tools:

- **No advance warning** of congestion before it becomes critical
- **Reactive planning** — operators respond to congestion instead of preventing it
- **Manual berth allocation** — time-consuming and prone to conflicts
- **No systematic crane optimization** — crane shortfalls are discovered too late
- **No 72-hour operations roadmap** — shift supervisors lack structured forward plans

### Required Capabilities (from problem statement)

1. **Predict congestion hotspots** using vessel schedules and berth capacity data
2. **Recommend alternate routing strategies** when terminals are at capacity
3. **Optimize berth and crane assignments** to maximize throughput and minimize waiting
4. **Generate a 72-hour port operations plan** for shift supervisors

### Operational Impact

Unmanaged congestion causes:
- Extended vessel waiting times (additional fuel costs, charter penalties)
- Reduced terminal throughput and revenue
- Supply chain disruptions for importers/exporters
- Reduced port competitiveness

### Our Approach

PortMind AI addresses all four requirements through a unified PREDICT → EXPLAIN → OPTIMIZE → PLAN workflow, making it the first platform to connect these stages into a single operational command center.
