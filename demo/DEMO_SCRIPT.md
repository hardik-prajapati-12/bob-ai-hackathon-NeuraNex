# PortMind AI — Demo Script

## Demo Journey (5 minutes)

### Setup
Ensure the application is running with seed data loaded.

### Step 1 — Login (30s)
Navigate to http://localhost:5173
Login: admin / portmind2026

### Step 2 — Dashboard (45s)
- Show: Rising congestion KPIs
- Point out: HIGH risk vessels, active alerts
- Highlight: Congestion trend chart showing Terminal 1 approaching critical

### Step 3 — Congestion Analytics (60s)
- Navigate to Congestion Analytics
- Show: 12H prediction for Terminal 1 — HIGH/CRITICAL
- Expand contributing factors: "Berth utilization 91%, 14 vessels in 6-hour window"
- Click "Run AI Explanation" to show watsonx.ai root-cause analysis

### Step 4 — Optimization Center (60s)
- Navigate to Optimization Center
- Show: Current plan vs AI-Recommended plan
- Highlight vessel V-102: current B3 → recommended B7 (reason shown)
- Show crane reallocation recommendations

### Step 5 — AI Operations Copilot (45s)
- Navigate to AI Assistant
- Ask: "Which vessels are at highest risk in the next 12 hours?"
- Show: Grounded response naming real vessels with data
- Ask: "Generate a 72-hour operations plan"

### Step 6 — Operations Plan (45s)
- Navigate to Operations Plan
- Show: Generated 72-hour plan with 4 time windows
- Highlight 0-12H immediate actions
- Show priority levels and expected benefits

### Step 7 — Vessel Management (30s)
- Navigate to Vessel Management
- Filter by HIGH/CRITICAL risk
- Show vessel detail panel

### Step 8 — Summary
- Return to Dashboard
- "PortMind AI: PREDICT → EXPLAIN → OPTIMIZE → PLAN"
